My work explores how AI systems can support human reasoning in healthcare and education. The projects below describe my current research; papers and code will be linked when publicly available.

<section class="research-entry" aria-labelledby="regulatutor">
  <h2 id="regulatutor">RegulaTutor</h2>
  <p class="project-subtitle">A theory-informed framework for long-term personalized learning</p>
  <p class="project-meta">First author · Manuscript in preparation</p>
  <p>RegulaTutor connects historical learner evidence, learner interpretation, and personalized instructional policies through a cognitive, affective, metacognitive, and motivational (CAMM) framework.</p>
  <p>I designed the framework and developed PairRoute, an offline refinement procedure that uses intermediate execution records to distinguish system-level, model-level, and mixed failures. My work also includes evaluation across model configurations, focusing on evidence accuracy, learner diagnosis, and teaching quality.</p>
</section>

<section class="research-entry" aria-labelledby="aividence">
  <h2 id="aividence">AIvidence</h2>
  <p class="project-subtitle">Reconciling patient-specific AI explanations with evidence-based medicine for clinical diagnosis</p>
  <p class="project-meta">Second author</p>
  <p>AIvidence is a visualization system that connects patient-specific AI explanations with biomedical literature to support clinical diagnostic decision-making.</p>
  <p>I contributed to system design and frontend and backend implementation, manuscript preparation, and an iterative design process informed by expert feedback and clinical requirements.</p>
</section>

<section class="research-entry" aria-labelledby="argumentation">
  <h2 id="argumentation">Argumentation-based human-AI interaction</h2>
  <p class="project-subtitle">Supporting daily healthcare decision-making</p>
  <p class="project-meta">Project lead · Ongoing research</p>
  <p>I am exploring how Toulmin's model of argumentation can make AI-assisted daily healthcare decisions more transparent and trustworthy. This project investigates argument structure as an interface for understanding and examining AI-generated health advice.</p>
</section>
